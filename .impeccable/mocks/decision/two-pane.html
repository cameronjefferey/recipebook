Crispy Skillet Turkey Burgers

Serves 4 · cooked 12× · 1×
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground turkey
  • ⅓ cup panko
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 4 buns, toasted
  • Butter lettuce, tomato

Steps

  1. 1Stir panko and mayonnaise into a paste; let sit two minutes.
  2. 2Fold in turkey, Worcestershire and salt with a fork. Do not overwork.
  3. 3Shape four patties a little wider than the buns.
  4. 4Heat a skillet over medium-high until a drop of water skips.
  5. 5Cook 5 minutes a side without pressing. Rest on the buns.
